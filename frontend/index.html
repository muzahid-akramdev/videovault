Facebook and Instagram block automated access — they need proof you're logged in. The safest way is to export your browser cookies from a tab where you're already logged in, then paste them here. Your cookies stay on your own device (saved in your Termux home folder) and are never sent anywhere except to Facebook/Instagram itself via yt-dlp.

How to get your Facebook cookies (phone/PC browser):
  • 01Install the Cookie-Editor browser extension (available for Chrome, Firefox, Edge).
  • 02Open facebook.com in that browser and make sure you are logged in.
  • 03Click the Cookie-Editor icon → click Export → choose Netscape format → Copy.
  • 04Paste the copied text into the box below and click Save Session.
